Demographic profile of all 1,762 populated California ZIP codes: household income, homeownership, home values, age and vehicles, built from the U.S. Census Bureau's ACS 2020 to 2024 five-year estimates (released December 2025), still the most current ZIP-level data published. Your GOAL leads come from across California, but most of them come from Southern California, and above all from Los Angeles County: of the 1,944 leads in your top eight counties, 1,297 (66.7%) are in Los Angeles County, and nine of your ten top lead cities are Los Angeles County cities. So this report leads with Los Angeles County, then the Inland Empire (San Bernardino and Riverside), Orange, San Diego and Sacramento, and keeps the statewide view for context. Together your eight lead counties form your lead footprint: 771 ZIPs and 61.0% of California households. Your Walnut Creek office sits in Contra Costa County, outside that footprint, so the market described here is mostly one your team serves by phone. Section 07 profiles each lead county and city side by side. The companion spreadsheet carries every ZIP and every column, with your lead counties flagged; the wildfire report covers the risk geography.

  • SourceU.S. Census Bureau, American Community Survey 2020 to 2024 five-year estimates (released December 2025), tables B01002, B01003, B08201, B11001, B11007, B19013, B25001, B25002, B25003, B25004, B25077. Geography: 2020 ZIP Code Tabulation Areas. The next five-year release (2021 to 2025) is scheduled for December 2026; these pages will be refreshed when it lands.
  • Coverage1,762 populated California ZIPs. PO-box-only ZIPs have no resident population and no Census profile; traffic from them resolves to the surrounding ZIP. Median income is published for 1,590 ZIPs and median home value for 1,599.
  • Top-codingThe Census caps medians at “$250,000 or more” for income (17 ZIPs) and “$2,000,000 or more” for home value (68 ZIPs); those ZIPs are shown at the cap.
  • GeographyEach ZIP is assigned to the California county holding the largest share of its land (2020 ZCTA-to-county relationship file). The 58 counties are grouped into nine regions: Los Angeles County, the Inland Empire (Riverside and San Bernardino), Orange County, San Diego & Imperial, the Central Coast (including Ventura), the Bay Area (including Contra Costa), the Sacramento Region, the San Joaquin Valley (including Kern), and the North State & Sierra. Density for the renter-core screen uses 2020 ZCTA land area.
  • LeadsYour lead geography as provided for GOAL account #927, statewide California and mostly Southern California: top cities Los Angeles (331), Long Beach (53), Lancaster (36), Palmdale (35), Whittier (33), Sacramento (31), Inglewood (26), Van Nuys (25), Beverly Hills (24) and Glendale (24); top counties Los Angeles (1,297), San Bernardino (172), Riverside (137), Orange (137), San Diego (82), Sacramento (54), Ventura (35) and Kern (30). Lead counts by ZIP were not part of this edition, so ZIP-level lead counts are not shown.
  • PrecisionAll values carry ACS margins of error and are strongest for larger ZIPs. Blank means not published, never zero.
